On February 23, I wrote that the open frontier was a historical anomaly — that access controls would ratchet, not loosen, and that national security would be the frame when they did.

Sixteen weeks later, on June 12, the U.S. Commerce Department issued an export-control directive cutting off foreign-national access to Anthropic's two most powerful models, Fable 5 and Mythos 5. Anthropic pulled them for all customers to comply.

I take no pleasure in the timing.

The three forces I laid out are no longer hypothetical. The advice hasn't changed either: Opus-class access is still open, and the teams that build during this window carry the head start into the restricted era.
