# [Generative AI - Digital Twins for Proactive and Cost-Effective Enterprise Cybersecurity Incident Response Testing](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/generative-ai-digital-twins-proactive-enterprise-incident-cravino-33jwe)

Created on 2023-12-22 16:33

Published on 2023-12-22 17:21

As cyber threats continue to evolve at an alarming rate, organizations must stay ahead of the curve by adopting innovative solutions like **digital twins** powered by Generative AI. Adversaries are already leveraging generative AI to launch sophisticated attacks, and we cannot afford to lag behind. In this article, we'll explore how generative AI-powered digital twins can help enterprises improve their incident response testing capabilities, save costs, and enhance overall security posture.

### Background:

I remember when I was at the University of Buenos Aires, I was amazed by a really old but amazing piece of software GPSS/PC - General Purpose Simulation System for PC ([wiki link](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPSS)) . It allowed me to create models of real-life systems, play with time-shifting, understand queues, response times, traffic, and 'What-if' scenarios. This tool was invaluable for planning ahead and making informed decisions when designing systems, regardless of the industry. I could predict faults, system breakdowns, and identify the best time for preventive maintenance.

Fast forward to 2023, and we now have Generative AI accessible to everyone. New and amazing conversational agent frameworks like Microsoft Autogen ([link](https://github.com/microsoft/autogen)), small/medium and large LLMs (Large Language Models) under the Apache 2 license, and API-based SaaS services like Azure OpenAI are available.

As an early adopter of Gen Ai, I cannot help but wonder how much better it could get if we take advantage of Generative AI in the Cybersecurity Incident Response Testing area.

### Why now?

Cyber threats are becoming increasingly sophisticated and frequent. Adversaries are leveraging generative AI to launch attacks that are difficult to detect and respond to.

> By using digital twins powered by Generative AI, organizations can simulate various incidents before they occur, helping them better prepare for potential threats and minimize the impact of actual events.

### Proposed Approach:

This proactive approach could allow organizations to identify weaknesses in their current incident response processes and develop strategies to address them effectively.

1. **Pick an area**: As a former boss use to say '*Do not try to boil the ocean*'; instead, start small by running a PoC on a specific area in your organization.

2. **Setting up the Simulation environment**:

* Create virtual replicas of the organization's **People,** **Process**, and **Technology** using conversational agent-based frameworks like MS Autogen ([link](https://github.com/microsoft/autogen)).
* Create RAGs (Retrieval Augmented Generation) from the current runbooks so that conversational agents can refer (through the LLMs) to the RAGs exclusively, to respond & react to inputs (and be able to say '*I don't know how to proceed*' i.e. 'fail' when there is no pertinent procedure in the runbook / RAG).
* Effortlessly generate as many scenarios as needed using generative AI, taking source Mitre Att&ck Threat Actors + Techniques and the latest Cyber Threat intel pertinent to your specific organization.

> The beauty of this approach is that it does not consume cycles from the real 'people' or 'tech.'

### Benefits:

*Improved Preparedness:* With a digital twin, you can simulate various incidents before they occur, helping your organization better prepare for potential threats and minimize the impact of actual events. This proactive approach allows you to identify weaknesses in your current incident response processes and develop strategies to address them effectively.

*Enhanced Decision-Making*: By analyzing simulation results, we can make more informed decisions about incident response strategies, resource allocation, and other critical aspects of their cybersecurity program. This data-driven approach enables us to allocate resources efficiently and maximize the effectiveness of our incident response efforts.

*Continuous Improvement*: Digital twins provide valuable insights into areas where your organization's IR process could be improved, allowing for ongoing refinements and enhancements. By regularly updating and maintaining your digital twin model, you can stay ahead of evolving threats and adapt your incident response processes as needed.

### Challenges:

* *Initial Setup Costs and lack of SMEs* on this area within the organizations.
* *Data Quality Concerns*: The accuracy of the digital twin depends heavily on the quality and completeness of the underlying data. Inaccurate or incomplete data can lead to misleading simulation results, which could negatively impact decision-making.
* *Maintenance Requirements*: Digital twins require regular updates and maintenance to ensure they remain accurate and relevant.

### Conclusion:

As the threat landscape continues to evolve, it's crucial for us to adopt innovative solutions like digital twins to strengthen our Cyber Security capabilities. By leveraging these virtual replicas of your ***People, Infrastructure, and Processes***, you could unlock new opportunities for preparedness, decision-making, and continuous improvement.
