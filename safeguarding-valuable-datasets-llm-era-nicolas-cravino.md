# [Safeguarding Valuable Datasets in the LLM Era](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/safeguarding-valuable-datasets-llm-era-nicolas-cravino)

Created on 2023-06-23 01:18

Published on 2023-06-23 01:49

In the ever-changing field of artificial intelligence, the significance of high-quality and unique datasets for training Language Models (LLMs) cannot be emphasized enough. As LLMs continue to transform various industries, one crucial aspect remains indispensable - access to exceptional datasets. However, with the increasing recognition of data's value, there has been a restriction in access, leading platforms like Reddit and Twitter to charge for their APIs. While safeguarding these valuable data sources is crucial, it is equally important to assess the potential risks linked to this trend. Lets explore this last bit.

The Growing Threat of Malicious Actors:

The pursuit of valuable datasets raises concerns about new emerging security threats. Bad actors may resort to illicit means to acquire such information. These datasets could include for example (i) A Knowledge Base with decades of invaluable data collected and curated by the organization, (ii)Proprietary methodologies (iii) Proposals and Client Deliverable archives, (iv) Proprietary Training Programs, etc. These type of datasets hold immense value for training LLMs. This concern becomes even more relevant with the rise of LoRA. Could this trend inadvertently lead to an increase in attempts to steal structured data?

The Emergence of a New Wave: From Ransomware to Data Theft:

Traditionally, ransomware attacks aim to profit from decryption fees. However, as structured datasets gain importance, there is a legitimate concern that malware attacks may potentially shift their focus to the underground market value of such information (structured Datasets). Bad actors may increasingly target knowledge bases, ticketing systems, and proprietary data collected across decades. The potential consequences could extend far beyond financial losses, impacting a company's competitive advantage at its core.

The Vital Role of Structured Datasets (LLM unsupervised training accelerators):

Success in the realm of LLMs depends on various factors. However, one key differentiator lies in structured and curated datasets utilized for unsupervised training. This essential element (aka "the secret sauce"), plays a pivotal role in achieving accurate and meaningful outcomes. As organizations tackle the complexities of protecting their assets, the focus must expand to safeguarding the structured datasets that could have been ignored previously.

Takeaway: Organizations need to understand that once the secrets are out, it is game over. Leaked quality structured datasets could be used to train and fine tune new models that could ‘clone’ the capabilities of an organization. This could result on having a new competitor that can deliver same quality as you, but for a fraction of the price or even for free if made available as Open source.

Uncovering Vulnerabilities: Key Questions to Consider:

In the face of these potential threats, organizations must carefully evaluate their approach to protecting their structured datasets. Contemplating the following questions can shed light on their security posture:

1. What would be the impact if our proprietary structured datasets were leaked?

2. Are our publicly available API's monitored against excessive usage ?

3- Do these proprietary datasets separate us from our peers, make us unique, is it a competitive advantage that requires extra monitoring and protection?

3. How would our peers / competitors benefit if they gained access to these valuable datasets in its entirety ?

4. What if this data became publicly available on platforms like Huggingface or Github?

Lessons from Algorithmic Trading

The consequences of uncontrolled accessibility to data and feeds are apparent in the field of algorithmic trading and Robo-advising. What was once a niche capability it might now become openly available through platforms like GitHub as open-source projects (check this week's top trending Open Source projects in GitHub,[FinGPT](https://github.com/trending) for example ). The driving force behind their exposure in the public domain was the exploitation of publicly available structured datasets and APIs. This serves as a reminder to protect the structured datasets before they are copied.

Conclusion: Taking Action to Safeguard Valuable Assets:

Recognizing that structured datasets are the foundation of successful LLMs, organizations must reassess their strategies to safeguard these invaluable assets before are ingested by our competitors. This includes identifying, categorizing, and implementing robust protection mechanisms to prevent unauthorized and or excessive access. Even if complete restriction is not feasible, controlling access to structured data becomes imperative to secure the organization's valuable differentiators hidden within these datasets.

In light of the evolving dynamics of data accessibility and the associated risks, it is crucial to act now. By identifying and then prioritizing the protection of structured datasets, organizations can navigate the inevitable and exponentially evolving LLM landscape with confidence, ensuring the preservation of their competitive edge.
