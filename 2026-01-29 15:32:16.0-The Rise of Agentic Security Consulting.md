# The Rise of Agentic Security Consulting

Created on 2026-01-29 15:32

Published on ---

*From Billable Hours to Outcome-Driven Trust*

A while back (April 2023), I explored how AI was reshaping [Cybersecurity Advisory](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/revolutionizing-cyber-risk-advisory-services-ai-nicolas-cravino) Services. Today we go deeper—into the core of how 'value' is measured, since the old model is shifting.

For decades, consulting was transactional: more hours = bigger invoice. But AI is changing the equation. Forward-thinking firms now ask: what if clients paid for delivered outcomes instead of effort?

Here's what that transformation looks like.

### From Hours to Impact

AI doesn't slow down—it accelerates. When a threat detection process that took 40 hours of human analysis can now be handled in minutes, productivity is no longer measured in billable hours. It's measured in speed, precision, and real results: reducing mean-time-to-respond from days to hours, cutting remediation backlogs, hitting measurable KPIs.

This shifts client expectations. They want outcomes—not effort.

### Outcome-Based Pricing: Aligning Incentives

Here's the model: clients pay when you deliver measurable impact. Reduce their risk by 30%? They pay. Accelerate threat mitigation from 72 hours to 8 hours? They pay. Hit agreed KPIs—fewer breaches, faster detection, lower compliance gaps?—success is mutual.

This builds trust because incentives align. Your success is inseparable from theirs. No more guessing whether the engagement delivered value.

### Real Example: From Hours to Outcomes

Consider a financial services firm managing 500+ assets. Historically, threat assessment was manual—weeks of consulting hours, predictable costs, unpredictable outcomes. Now: AI-powered agents handle continuous risk profiling, flag anomalies for human review, escalate critical issues in real-time. Result: 60% faster risk identification, 40% reduction in undetected threats. The client pays for that delivered security posture, not the labor behind it.

### Oversight: AI at Speed, With Human Guardrails

This isn't blind workflow automation. Real security requires layers of responsibility: Agents handle routine analysis and monitoring autonomously. Anomalies trigger escalation to human dashboards immediately. Humans remain in control—defining boundaries, making critical decisions, and hitting the circuit breaker if needed.

It's AI at machine speed, backed by human judgment. The best of both worlds.

### Digital Twins: Encoding Institutional Knowledge

The Big Four have an edge: decades of expertise embedded in processes, playbooks, and lessons learned. By creating AI-powered digital twins fine-tuned on their internal knowledge, they scale their best minds 24/7. These aren't generic models—they encode what worked, what failed, and why.

![](https://media.licdn.com/dms/image/v2/D4E12AQEJ-NMdq9cagg/article-inline_image-shrink_1500_2232/B4EZwKv.FBKwAU-/0/1769706895487?e=1776297600&v=beta&t=VsT6Vf6dmGHX-9HMKmaQSkCiyMSiXTNI-RNjqhkEgI8)

Over time, this builds something powerful: a "[user.md](http://user.md)" for each client. The longer the partnership, the more tailored and predictive the guidance becomes. They anticipate issues before they surface because they understand your risk profile deeply.

### Proprietary Security Infrastructure: The Real Differentiator

The Big Four should be building proprietary evaluation frameworks—tools that go beyond off-the-shelf security scanning. Consider this example that personally designed (and is [available in Github](https://github.com/ai-agents-cybersecurity/TSLIT)) what defense-grade AI validation looks like: comprehensive LLM integrity testing harnesses that detect time-based logic bombs and affiliation bias in AI models.

Imagine testing whether a model behaves differently based on the requester's nation-state affiliation AND the date—catching sophisticated conditional malice that only activates on symbolic dates like 9/11 or July 4th for specific affiliations. That's Stuxnet-level threat detection !. That's the kind of infrastructure that separates true expertise from commodity advisory.

The edge isn't just using AI. It's building the tools to validate it.

### The Edge on Lock-In

Transfer knowledge? Yes. Consultancies train teams, document strategies, ensure clients can move if they choose. But here's what's irreplaceable: Consultancies don't stop innovating. While a departing client starts from that baseline, they're continuously researching threats, refining models, and accessing emerging tools before they're mainstream. Their evolving expertise ensures clients know—if you want to stay ahead, partnership with Big4s keeps you there.

### Multi-Model Partnerships: No Single Point of Failure

Big4s should not rely on just one AI provider (Hyper-Scaler). Anthropic, OpenAI, Google—each has unique strengths. By orchestrating multiple model providers, Big4s could ensure the right tool for each job (strategic analysis, threat detection, code review, incident response), built-in resilience (if one provider has issues, they failover seamlessly), and always cutting-edge solutions, never locked to yesterday's tech.

Clients gain both operational continuity and continuous access to what's next.

### Early Access to Tomorrow's Tech

The Big Four partner directly with hyper-scalers. They don't just implement emerging tools—they **co-develop** them. This insider track means clients access innovation before it's mainstream. Consultancies are not implementing what's known today; they're building what's next.

### Assurance Through Rigorous Governance

Outcome-based pricing requires **trust**. That trust comes from: continuous validation (every AI output is benchmarked against ground truth and known results), compliance frameworks (ISO 27001, SOC 2, NIST—plus AI-specific governance ensuring responsible use), and full audit trails (every decision is traceable, we explain how conclusions were reached, what data informed it, and why). Furthermore, the Big4s are always preparing for emerging regulatory requirements, they are on the ball.

No black boxes. Full transparency.

### Institutional Memory as Competitive Advantage

Here's what separates Big4s: AI can synthesize ***every past engagement***, **every lesson learned**, **every strategic decision**. Big4s see patterns across your industry peers (respecting confidentiality) that inform their advisory. They know which controls work, which fail, and why. That institutional memory-combined with continuous innovation-gives them an unmatched edge.

They don't just offer expertise. They offer the full history of what worked and why.

### The Real Product: Trust and Outcomes

Clients aren't buying AI. They're buying **reduced risk**, **measurable impact**, and a trusted **partner** who ensures they're always ahead of the curve.

The future of security consulting isn't cheaper—**it's smarter**. It's not fewer people—it's people **amplified and augmented by AI**. It's not less accountability—it's more transparency and alignment.

The Big Four aren't replacing traditional consulting. They're evolving it. They're defining what security advisory becomes in an AI-native world.
